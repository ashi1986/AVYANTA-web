AI Implementation

AI implementation services, from use case to production

Most AI projects stall between the pilot and real use. AVYANTA is an AI implementation partner that closes that gap: we pick the right use cases, prepare the data, integrate AI into your existing systems and stay until it delivers measurable results.

What we implement

We implement AI where it removes the most manual work and connects directly to the systems your business already runs on.

AI for finance & accounting

Invoice processing, reconciliations, GST and compliance checks, and reporting automated with AI agents connected to your accounting system.

AI for demand planning

Forecasting, inventory and S&OP workflows implemented on your sales and supply data, with outputs your planners can act on.

AI for operations

Order processing, approvals, document handling and status updates automated across ERP, email and WhatsApp.

LLM & RAG implementation

Secure enterprise assistants grounded in your documents and data, deployed on your cloud or on-premise.

Computer vision implementation

Vision models deployed on cameras and edge devices for inspection, safety and process automation.

AI integration & MLOps

APIs, data pipelines, monitoring and retraining so AI runs reliably inside your existing technology stack.

Our AI implementation process

  1. 1

    Assess

    Review processes, systems and data readiness; prioritise use cases by value and feasibility.

  2. 2

    Pilot

    Implement one use case end to end on real data with clear success metrics.

  3. 3

    Integrate

    Connect the AI to ERP, CRM, accounting and messaging systems with proper security and access control.

  4. 4

    Roll out

    Train teams, change workflows, and scale to more processes and locations.

  5. 5

    Operate

    Monitor accuracy and cost, handle edge cases and improve models over time.

  6. 6

    Measure

    Track hours saved, turnaround times and cost against the baseline agreed at the start.

Frequently asked questions

What are AI implementation services?

AI implementation services take an AI use case from idea to everyday use: selecting the use case, preparing data, building or configuring models, integrating them with business systems, training users and supporting the system in production.

How long does an AI implementation take?

A first use case typically goes from assessment to pilot in a few weeks, with production rollout following in phases depending on integrations and scale.

Do we need clean data before starting?

No. Data readiness is part of our implementation process; we assess what you have and build the pipelines needed.

Can you integrate AI with our existing ERP or accounting software?

Yes. We integrate AI agents and models with ERP, CRM, accounting, email and WhatsApp through APIs and secure connectors.

Do you provide AI implementation in Bengaluru?

Yes. AVYANTA's team is in Bengaluru, India, and can run on-site workshops and rollouts for clients in the city and across India.
