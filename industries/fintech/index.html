Why now

A lending book runs on three numbers. Most of what moves them is still done by hand.

People reading statements, calling borrowers and compiling returns. We build the AI that does the reading, scoring, calling and explaining, inside the LOS, LMS and dialler you already run.

Approval rate

Thin-file salaried borrowers get declined on bureau alone while the bank statements and payslips that would approve them go unread.

Credit loss

Edited PDFs, synthetic identities and mule rings pass KYC and OCR. The loss surfaces months later as a first-payment default.

Cost to collect

Bucket 0 to 30 is worked by dialler teams. Cost rises with the book, and tone on recorded calls is a conduct risk under RBI rules.

Offerings 01 to 04

Underwriting and risk

Approve more of the right borrowers, and see the slip before it happens

The biggest prize in a personal-loan or SME book is the thin-file borrower you decline today and the stressed account you see too late.

01

Alternative-data credit model

The problemBureau-only scorecards decline thin-file borrowers who would repay.

What we buildGradient-boosted and graph models on bank flows, UPI behaviour, employer stability, device signals and bureau data, trained and validated on your own disbursed and rejected book. Served as an API to your LOS with reason codes on every decision.

  • Higher approvals at the same loss
  • Cash-flow scoring for thin files
  • Reason codes for credit and RBI
02

Bank statement and payslip intelligence

The problemStatements arrive from dozens of banks in dozens of layouts, and analysts miss edited files.

What we buildLLM plus OCR pipeline that parses any Indian bank statement into clean transactions, with tamper and synthetic-statement detection at pixel and transaction level. Salary regularity, EMI load, bounce history and lender exposure from uploaded PDFs or Account Aggregator feeds.

  • Minutes per statement, any bank
  • Tampered PDFs flagged before credit
  • One pipeline for PDF and AA data
03

Employer risk engine with EPFO (PF) verification

The problemA salaried loan is really an employer loan, yet whether the employer is real, and still paying PF for this borrower, goes unchecked.

What we buildVerifies that the declared employer exists and is active, and that the applicant appears in its EPFO (PF) contributions. On top of that, a continuous employer health score from MCA filings, EPFO contribution data, hiring and layoff news and salary-delay patterns in your own book. Watchlists and category caps that update themselves, used in underwriting and portfolio monitoring.

  • Employer and employment verified from PF data
  • Live employer score with early warning
  • Exposure by employer and sector
04

Early warning system

The problemThe first signal of stress is a bounced EMI, which is already a DPD.

What we buildA model that predicts the probability of a missed EMI 30 days ahead from repayment history, bank behaviour, app engagement and employer score. Daily risk tiers pushed into the LMS and collections queue, with pre-emptive nudges and restructure offers before the due date.

  • 30-day head start on slips
  • Pre-emptive outreach
  • Lower roll-forward to bucket 30
Offerings 05 to 09

Fraud and KYC

Catch the fraud that passes KYC

Deepfakes, reused photos, synthetic identities and mule rings are now the main source of first-payment default in digital lending. Five offerings stop them before disbursal and keep Aadhaar data compliant.

05

Aadhaar and PAN detection in milliseconds

The problemEvery upload has to be sorted and checked for Aadhaar and PAN by hand, which slows onboarding and lets mismatches through.

What we buildVision models that find and classify Aadhaar and PAN cards inside any photo, scan or multi-page PDF, read the fields, validate format and checksum, and cross-check them against the applicant's details. Responds in milliseconds, so it runs inline in the onboarding flow.

  • Millisecond response, inline in onboarding
  • Fields extracted and validated
  • Mismatches flagged before KYC
06

Aadhaar masking across scanned loan files

The problemLoan against property and secured loan files carry full Aadhaar numbers across hundreds of scanned pages, and UIDAI rules require them masked.

What we buildFinds every Aadhaar number, card image and QR code across scanned agreements, property papers and KYC packs, and masks the first eight digits before the file is stored or shared. Works on the existing back-book in bulk as well as on new files, with a page-level audit log.

  • UIDAI-compliant masking at scale
  • Back-book cleaned in bulk
  • Page-level audit log
07

Deepfake selfie and document detection

The problemSelfies, video KYC and uploaded documents are being beaten by face-swaps, replay attacks and AI-generated or edited files.

What we buildLiveness detection, face match and injection-attack detection trained on Indian ID formats and lighting conditions, plus detection of AI-generated and edited documents at pixel and layout level. Runs on-premise or in your VPC so biometric data never leaves your control.

  • 99.7% face verification accuracy
  • Deepfake selfies and forged documents caught
  • Biometric data stays with you
08

Fraud photo match across applications

The problemThe same face or the same document photo reappears under different names, and nothing compares it across applications.

What we buildFace and image embeddings for every selfie and document photo, searched against your full application history in milliseconds. Flags the same person under a new identity, reused document images and known-fraud faces, with the matched applications shown side by side.

  • Repeat fraudsters caught on reapply
  • Search across your whole book
  • Matches shown side by side
09

Synthetic identity, mule and transaction fraud detection

The problemFraud rings apply as dozens of clean-looking individuals, and anomalies are flagged after the money has moved.

What we buildLink analysis across phone, device, email, address, bank account and employer to find clusters. Graph features and millisecond-scale velocity rules scored at application, payout and across live transaction networks, with an analyst console that shows the linked entities behind every flag.

  • Rings caught at the second application
  • Payout accounts screened for mule patterns
  • Millisecond anomaly flagging
Offerings 10 to 12

Collections

The fastest return in the stack is collections

Collections shows a measurable number within 60 days without touching your credit policy. Three offerings that cut cost to collect and conduct risk together.

10

Voice AI collection agents

The problemBucket 0 to 30 reminders are worked by human dialler teams, and coverage drops at month end.

What we buildVoice agents in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Hinglish and English that remind, capture promise-to-pay and offer settlements within approved bands. Guardrails for calling hours, language and escalation are built in, with hand-off to a human the moment the borrower asks or disputes.

  • 100% of bucket 0 to 30 reached
  • Fraction of dialler cost per contact
  • Compliant by design
11

Collections prioritisation model

The problemAgents call lists in order of amount or DPD, not likelihood to pay.

What we buildA model that ranks each delinquent account by propensity to pay and recoverable amount, then recommends channel (voice AI, WhatsApp, human, field), best time and settlement offer. Feeds the dialler and the voice agents directly.

  • Right call, right borrower, right time
  • 5 to 15% typical lift in roll-back
  • Higher recovery per agent hour
12

Agent call QA

The problemQA listens to a small sample of recovery calls by ear, and harassment-risk language is found only after a complaint.

What we buildTranscription of every call in Indian languages, compliance scoring against RBI recovery guidelines and your script, and daily flags, agent scorecards and coaching clips for team leads.

  • 100% of calls scored
  • Same-day flag on conduct risk
  • Evidence for RBI and ombudsman

Vernacular loan assistant

What we buildAn assistant in the app and on WhatsApp that answers in the borrower's language, with tool-calling into your LOS and LMS for eligibility, document status, EMI, foreclosure and statements. Escalates to a human with the full context attached.

  • 24x7 support in Indian languages
  • Fewer drop-offs at the document step
  • Lower cost per resolved query
14

Personalised offer engine

The problemTop-up and pre-approved limits run as monthly batch campaigns that ignore current cash-flow and risk.

What we buildReal-time decisions on limit, top-up, rate and tenure for each borrower, using repayment behaviour, bank flows and the early-warning score. Delivered in-app, on WhatsApp and through the assistant.

  • Real-time offers, not monthly batches
  • Higher top-up take-up
  • Risk-priced limits

Operations and compliance

Credit ops and compliance, with people approving only exceptions

Four offerings that turn documents in any language into decisions, filings and audit trails.

15

Multilingual OCR for any document

The problemPayslips, property papers, ITRs and handwritten forms arrive in English, Hindi and regional scripts, and generic OCR fails on them.

What we buildDeep-learning OCR that reads printed and handwritten text in English and major Indian scripts across scans, phone photos and PDFs, and returns structured fields with confidence scores. It feeds every other offering on this page, from statement parsing to Aadhaar masking.

  • English and Indian scripts
  • Handwriting and phone photos
  • Structured fields with confidence scores
16

Agentic credit ops copilot

The problemA credit analyst opens bureau, statement, payslip and KYC in four tabs and types the memo by hand.

What we buildOne agent that pulls bureau, statement analysis, employer score, fraud flags and KYC into a one-page credit memo with a recommended decision checked against your policy. The analyst edits and approves, and every step is logged.

  • One-page memo in seconds
  • Consistent decisions across shifts
  • Faster manual-review turnaround
17

Regulatory reporting and finance document automation

The problemRBI returns and co-lending reports are compiled by hand, and invoices, expense claims and contracts are keyed in or scanned manually.

What we buildAutomated RBI returns and co-lending reconciliation with partner banks. Document AI for invoice reconciliation, expense management and vendor bills: extract, validate and post to the ERP. Clause-level regulatory scanning of contracts and policies.

  • 85% less manual data entry
  • Reconciled co-lending books every cycle
  • Audit trail from filing to source
18

Model risk and explainability layer

The problemRBI's digital lending guidelines expect explainable, fair decisions, and model drift goes unnoticed between annual validations.

What we buildReason codes on every score with SHAP-style attributions in plain language, drift, stability and fairness monitoring on live decisions, and a model inventory with validation reports and an audit console.

  • Every decline explained
  • Continuous monitoring
  • Ready for RBI, auditors and the board

FAQ

Questions lenders ask us

Which fintech and lending companies is this built for?+

Digital lenders and NBFCs offering personal, SME, gold and vehicle loans, co-lending platforms, payment companies and wealth platforms in India. The offerings are modular, so a lender can start with collections or fraud and add underwriting later.

Does borrower, bureau or biometric data leave our environment?+

No. Every offering runs on-premise or inside your VPC. Models are trained on your data in your environment and you own the code, the models and the data.

How do you handle RBI digital lending and fair practice requirements?+

Every credit decision carries reason codes and a source trail, voice agents operate inside calling-hour, language and escalation guardrails, every recovery call is transcribed and scored, and the explainability layer monitors drift and fairness continuously.

What does a first engagement look like?+

Two weeks of discovery on real applications, statements and calls, then the highest-value offering live on a pilot segment in about eight weeks, measured against a control group. Pricing is fixed and milestone-based, with two months of support after go-live.

Which systems do you integrate with?+

Loan origination and management systems, bureaus, Account Aggregator frameworks, diallers and telephony, WhatsApp Business, core banking and ERP, through APIs and secure data exchange.
